Products & Programs

AI systems that support crops, soil, plants, and livestock operations.

Uniquefarmz is designed for farms that want clearer instructions, stronger monitoring routines, and better day-to-day control over what happens in the field and around livestock activity.

01

AI Notifications and Farm Instructions

Receive alerts and operational instructions that help workers know what to do, when to do it, and which farm area needs immediate attention.

  • Task timing reminders
  • Instruction-led field actions
  • Routine support for farm teams
  • Faster response to changing conditions

02

Soil Testing Support

Keep track of soil testing activities and use AI guidance to understand when checks should happen and what conditions may require intervention.

  • Testing schedule awareness
  • Field-specific monitoring support
  • Health visibility for crop zones
  • Better timing for soil decisions

03

Plant Condition Monitoring

Track crop and plant response, field changes, and signs of stress with AI-supported observations designed to help farmers respond with more precision.

  • Plant health observation support
  • Structured crop-check routines
  • Better field monitoring consistency
  • Helpful visibility for intervention planning

04

Livestock Tracking Boards

Organize livestock notes, behavior changes, feeding schedules, and status updates in one tracking environment that helps farms keep a clearer operational picture.

  • Livestock activity logging
  • Feeding and routine visibility
  • Animal monitoring boards
  • Better coordination across care tasks
